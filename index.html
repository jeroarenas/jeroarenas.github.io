About

I am Full Professor at the Department of Signal Theory and Communications of Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (UC3M), where I was Head of Department from 2023 to 2026, and a member of the Machine Learning for Data Science group. I received the Telecommunication Engineering degree from Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (2000, first of my class, National Degree Award) and the PhD from UC3M (2004, Extraordinary Doctorate Award) as an FPU fellow.

I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Technical University of Denmark for 15 months and have done research stays at the University of Sheffield, UCLA, IIT Kharagpur, the University of São Paulo and the Barcelona Supercomputing Center (2021, transformer-based neural topic models).

Research

My group builds machine learning and NLP systems, increasingly based on language models, that support decision-making by domain experts. We have worked on scientific production and research funding, on public procurement and, more recently, on clinical text. In each case the work goes beyond the processing pipeline to end-user tools that experts evaluate in their own workflows.

  • Topic models for decision support

    Neural topic models that are interpretable and usable by non-specialists, with expert-in-the-loop training and hierarchical refinement.

  • Language models for information extraction

    Local, open-weight language models for semantic search, data enrichment and schema-guided extraction from documents, with attention to reliability and explainability.

  • Foundations

    Multivariate analysis, kernel methods, adaptive filtering and, recently, estimation with known input covariance. A solid grasp of the foundations allows ad-hoc designs instead of off-the-shelf tools.

Systems & software

  • ITMT: Interactive Topic Model Trainer, for training and curating topic models with domain experts (EACL 2023 demo).
  • CASE: large-scale topic exploitation for decision support systems (COLING 2025 demo).
  • PONTE: NLP pipeline for public procurement data, being integrated into the procurement platform of the ALIA initiative.
  • IntelComp and nextProcurement: AI platforms for science, technology and innovation policy and for public procurement (European projects).
  • Research funding recommender: matches funding calls to UC3M researchers.
  • Legal-ES: large-scale resources for Spanish legal text processing.
